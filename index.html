Clip Hunter

Stream highlights, cut for Shorts.

Clip Hunter is a personal desktop tool used by the Meek's Moments channel. Its purpose is to find highlight moments in public Twitch streams, edit them into short vertical clips, and post the clips its owner approves to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account.

How it works

  1. Finds the moment. It reads a stream's public chat replay and the clips viewers made, and picks the moments people reacted to most.
  2. Edits the clip. It cuts each moment from the stream's own video into a 9:16 clip with captions, a title and a credit linking to the streamer's channel.
  3. Waits for review. The owner watches every clip and rates it. Only clips the owner approves are posted.
  4. Posts to the owner's accounts. Approved clips are uploaded to the owner's own YouTube channel (through the YouTube Data API), Facebook Page and Instagram account.

How it uses Google and YouTube

Clip Hunter uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API only on the account of its signed-in owner: to upload approved clips to that channel, check that each upload was processed, and read how those videos perform so it can make better clips. It never acts on anyone else's account. The privacy policy lists exactly what it accesses.

What Clip Hunter never does

  • It never generates, creates or alters images or video of people with AI. Every clip is a cut of the stream's original footage.
  • It never creates, edits or posts sexual or intimate imagery of anyone, real or synthetic. When nudity appears in a stream, Clip Hunter blurs it or leaves the moment out.
  • It does not post to other people's accounts, collect personal data, or sell or share any data.

Who it is for

Clip Hunter is not a public service. There are no accounts to create and nothing to sign up for. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by them.
